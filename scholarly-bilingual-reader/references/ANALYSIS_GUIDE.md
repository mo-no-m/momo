# Argument analysis

For each paragraph, distinguish its function (background, question, method category, result, interpretation, limitation or transition), logical position (connection to preceding and following claims), and evidence analysis (what the evidence supports and what it does not). Do not manufacture arguments. Preserve uncertainty, qualifiers, units and citation markers. Use yellow for claims, red for concepts, blue for evidence, green for caveats, purple for method descriptions where safe. Do not turn sensitive biological literature into actionable protocols or design guidance. Discuss scientific conclusions and limitations at an appropriate level. In the overview, identify the question, thesis, evidence chain, section roles, uncertainties and follow-up questions.
